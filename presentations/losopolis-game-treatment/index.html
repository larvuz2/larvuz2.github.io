LOSOPOLIS

A surreal co-op puzzle adventure inside an impossible tower.

Concept treatment · 2026
01
The core creative bet

The strongest asset is not conventional action.
It is the tower itself.

Losopolis should be a place players learn, distrust, and eventually manipulate: a living vertical world governed by strange physical rules.

05
Player 01 · physical reality

“There’s literally no door here.”

One player can physically manipulate Losopolis: pull, rotate, carry, brace, and break the room.

Player 02 · perceived reality

“There is on my screen.”

The other sees hidden paths, false geometry, or the creature that the first player cannot perceive.

06
The ingredient that makes it streamable

Controlled chaos between the puzzles.

The tower should be full of physical toys—not a chain of rigid logic tests. Make co-op failures feel dramatic, funny, and worth sharing.

Grab & save

Catch your partner before a fall, then both swing into the next space.

Push & throw

Objects are tools until they become a disaster.

Climb & dangle

Always give the body a way to improvise around the puzzle.

Ride the impossible

Loose furniture, creatures, and rooms become moving platforms.

Physics failure

Reversed gravity creates accidents players will clip immediately.

Krinkle encounter

Deception and misdirection, not conventional combat.

07
Why this is buildable

Make it feel enormous without building an open world.

  • One vertical destination with a strong identity.
  • Reusable movement and interaction systems.
  • Rules, rooms, and character chemistry—not content bloat.
  • High visual return from controlled environments.

08
Steam one-liner

A cinematic co-op puzzle adventure where two players climb an impossible tower whose rules of gravity, space, and reality change on every floor.

The tower is the hook. Co-op turns its weirdness into a language. The result can feel much larger than the budget.
