09 — Public Relations Plan10
The repeatable machinery

Build one campaign system, not four separate marketing problems.

01 — Master page

Release Run hub

One clean page with the four months, one-line story, current release, links, press images, and an email sign-up. This is the campaign’s home.

02 — Master asset

One 90-second film

A cinematic trailer for the whole release run. Use it at launch, then cut it into four monthly chapters and short social pieces.

03 — Master rhythm

Three beats per month

Tease → release → proof. Announce the reason it matters, release the thing, then show people responding to it or making with it.

10 — Micro Plan11
Every release repeats this

A 4-week public rhythm.

Keep it simple. The campaign feels alive when it has cadence, not when it posts constantly.

WEEK 01
Set the frameOne clear announcement: what is happening, why it matters, when people can enter.
WEEK 02
Show the makingOne process artifact: rehearsal, production, prototype, room, sketch, or decision behind the release.
WEEK 03
Release the heroThe trailer, event, game, song, or premiere moment. Give it one direct call to action.
WEEK 04
Show the proofAudience response, participant work, playthroughs, reviews, images, a short reflection—and point to next month.
